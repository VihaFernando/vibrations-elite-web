## What these licences do not cover

The Unsplash and Pexels licences cover the photographs, not the brands in them. The Pexels licence specifically prohibits implying that a brand endorses your product. The photographs show logos such as Apple, Samsung and JBL, and the Apple guidelines discourage third-party use of its product images and logo in marketing. The site does not claim any partnership or authorised status. Take advice if you are unsure, especially if the business is not an authorised reseller.

The Home hero shows the Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus logos at the owner's request. The shapes come from the CC0 simple-icons set, but CC0 does not grant trademark rights. Elsewhere, brand names appear as plain text.
